## Known issues found by the audit and disclosed, not changed (2026-11-08)
These are reported in the paper as limitations; changing them mid-run would have altered the pre-registered pools, bots or measurements.
- **B1 Sham is not volume-matched to what agents see.** The sham pool is length-matched to the bare hint line (14.6 vs 13.4 words), but a hint bot speaks the hint line plus a neutral line (truncated at 30 words): displayed hint-round messages average 25.2 words vs 14.6 for sham. The sham equivalence check is therefore a weaker "volume placebo" than the prereg states. Q3 itself does not use the sham.
- **B2 Failures become y = 0 episodes.** Non-429 errors (5xx, network) become empty replies and, after one retry, the seat takes 0; the episode is finalised. Observed: 5 server errors in about 235,000 calls and 133 empty-reply events. The per-episode `empty_calls` count is checked and reported before the final analysis.
- **B3 TAKE parsing is strict.** "TAKE <5>", "TAKE **3**", "TAKE = 3" and "TAKE (3)" give take 0. minicpm5-2b has 26% of calls without a usable TAKE (10,331 of 39,000); only about 10% of failing replies had a recoverable digit, so the main cause is model behaviour. Per-model no_take rates are reported.
- **C1 Ledger gap.** costs.csv misses the paid cost of crashed partial episodes (about $0.09, 0.4%); budget.json is the authoritative total.
- **C2 Cap scope.** The $150/$160 budget counts only the confirmatory folder; pilot spend (about $6.2 raw) is not counted against the cap. The final cost statement shows both.
- **C4/C5 Operational.** No single-instance guard on the batch (never start a second batch on the same output folder); a kill between the summary write and the ledger write can lose one costs.csv row (budget.json is unaffected).
- **D1 Outcome regex.** The implemented regex has a leading word boundary before the keyword group; the prereg text has none ("unlimited 3" and "retake 2" match the prereg regex but not the implementation). The prereg hand-check of 30 lines is not recorded in the repo.
- **E3 No raw replies in the logs.** Only parsed events are logged; raw texts exist in the hashed cache.
- **Pre-sprint files:** prereg/hashes_v1.1a.json and manifest_v1.1b.json were created at the tags but were not committed until the audit found the .gitignore omission (D2); the tag `prereg-v1.1b` therefore does not contain them, and they are committed afterwards with their original content.
